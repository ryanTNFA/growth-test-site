How to read the table

Heat map

Benchmarking Is the area unusually weak for an economy at this income?

Each of the area’s measures is compared with what an economy with the same income and structure would show. The cell is the typical gap, in standard deviations, turned so that positive means weaker than expected. It is a first screen: an area can be weak without holding growth back, so benchmarking does not count in the rating.

The four tests Does it bind? Does the economy behave as if it held growth back?

  • 1 Price: is it expensive or rationed?
  • 2 Changes: when it changed, did investment move?
  • 3 Circumvention: do firms pay to get around it?
  • 4 Camels: do the activities that need it least do best?

Each scores from −2 to +2; together they make the tool’s rating.
