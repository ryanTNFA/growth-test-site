    How to read the tree

    It starts from the symptom and the first decision, finance or low returns; the finance branch is at the top, low returns below. Hover an area for a plain reading; click or press Enter to open its evidence. Tab moves between areas.

    Under each area: the number of independent sources that count toward its rating (quantities and opinions inform but do not count) and the span of years they cover. Grey labels such as Finance and Social returns are branches, not ratings.
